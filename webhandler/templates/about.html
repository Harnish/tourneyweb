About TourneyWeb

TourneyWeb is an open-source tournament management app. Built originally for baseball, it works for any sport with scheduled games between two teams: divisions, teams, games, standings, and elimination brackets, all in one place.

Features

  • Multi-tournament, multi-division scheduling with per-team standings (wins, losses, runs)
  • Configurable tiebreaker rankings per division
  • Single- and double-elimination brackets with automatic seeding and score-driven advancement
  • Role-based access: directors, staff (score entry), and coaches (roster management)
  • Player rosters, team QR codes, and printable team sheets
  • Field/location maps, per-division rules, news, and freeform event pages

Open Source

TourneyWeb is free and open source, written in Go. Source code, issue tracker, and contribution guide live on GitHub:

github.com/Harnish/tourneyweb
